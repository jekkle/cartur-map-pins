# Map Pins 2.0.0 — drafts (nothing posted yet)

## Discord #announcements
**Cartur's Map Pins 2.0 is out on Thunderstore and Nexus.** Biggest update yet.
- **Hugin sets it up with you.** First load after installing, he lands and offers a menu: pick what gets pinned, with icons, colours and hover descriptions. `carturpins_setup` opens it again.
- **Pin colours.** Colour wheel with your last five colours, and every new pin takes the colour of what it marks (copper brown, each berry its own). One button colours the pins you already have.
- **New map-screen picker.** Drag it by the top bar, Visible to other players in the header, favourite stars with a Recent row, inventory-style hover pop.
- **H hides pins.** All, then only yours, then all back. Names go too.
- **Smooth zoom.** No more flicker or jumping pins.
- **Portals** are pinned with the name you gave them. Shift-click works on spawn and death markers.
- Optional: **clusters** with count badges, and **priority zoom-out**.
- Fixed: switching berries or mushrooms on while playing now pins what is already loaded.
Thanks to everyone who asked for these!
